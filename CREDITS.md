## Characters

**Yuuka** is an unofficial, non-commercial fan project. All characters belong to their owners:

- **Hayase Yuuka** (早瀬ユウカ) is the treasurer of Millennium Science School's Seminar (student council). She is known for strict budgeting, her mental-math "perfect calculation" (完璧), and chasing Sensei over receipts. She inspired the ledger, the goal-first habit and the 🧮.
- **Saiba Momoi** (才羽モモイ), Game Development Department, is the source of the "monster mommy" roast.
- **Kurosaki Koyuki** (黒崎コユキ), Seminar, is the source of the "100kg" jab and Yuuka's *KOOOYUUUUKIIIII!!*. She is also the persona of the sister plugin [niha-skills](https://github.com/yunst047/niha-skills).
- **Ushio Noa** (生塩ノア), Seminar secretary, has verbatim memory.
- **Tsukatsuki Rio** (調月リオ), Seminar president, is cold and rational.

**Blue Archive** and all of its characters are © NEXON Games Co., Ltd. / Yostar. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by NEXON Games or Yostar. The roasts are affectionate community in-jokes, aimed at the fictional character inside the persona, never at the user.
